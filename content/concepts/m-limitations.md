# Limitations

<p class="doc-meta"><span class="chip chip-concept">Concept</span> <span class="chip chip-project">Master</span> <span class="chip chip-date">25-07-2026</span></p>

What the results of this thesis cannot show.

- **Planned behaviour.** Participants followed instructions, so their behaviour was not fully natural. This matters most for the stops with small movements ([Group D: movement type](m-experiment-group-d.md)).
- **Several phones per stop.** The recordings of one stop (three for most stops, up to five) are not independent. [Grouped cross-validation](m-grouped-cross-validation.md) reduces this problem but does not remove it.
- **Terraces and balconies.** "Building-attached Outdoor" is counted as open space ([Self-report form](m-self-report-form.md)).
- **One street map.** The street results come partly from how this map cuts streets into pieces, so the street numbers belong to this map ([Candidate density bias toward building](../analysis/m-candidate-density-bias.md)).
- **Open-space errors.** The cause of the open-space errors has not been found.
- **Stays one by one.** Each stay is judged alone, without the trips before and after it.
- **No MILES test yet.** The model has not been used on MILES, so the risk described in [Overfitting signature](m-overfitting-signature.md) has not been tested.
