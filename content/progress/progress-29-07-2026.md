# Does downsampling matter?

<p class="doc-meta"><span class="chip chip-progress">Progress</span> <span class="chip chip-date">29-07-2026</span></p>

Sensei's question. The [Final model](../analysis/final-model.md) specification was refit on raw, non-downsampled ground truth.

- **No harm from downsampling:** downsampled CV 0.716 ± 0.076 vs raw 0.697 ± 0.062, condition number 1,373 vs 2,865 ([Raw-density refit](../analysis/raw-density-refit.md)).
- α recalibrates to 4.6159 on raw data, but on a much flatter profile ([Raw-density alpha profile](../analysis/raw-density-alpha-profile.md)).
- Side finding: downsampling also shrinks the candidate search area ([Downsampling shrinks the candidate search area](../analysis/candidate-search-area.md)).
