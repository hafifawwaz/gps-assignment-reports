Part of [Thesis summary](m-thesis-summary.md). Why this method is needed, and how it differs from methods that came before.

- [Motivation](../concepts/m-motivation.md): the usual "nearest building" method ignores streets and open spaces and ignores GPS error. Earlier probabilistic methods only work for people who are moving.
- [Research questions](../concepts/m-research-questions.md): the goal of the thesis and its three research questions.
- [Positioning against deterministic assignment](../concepts/m-positioning-vs-deterministic-assignment.md): methods that give one fixed answer, compared with this method, which gives probabilities.
- [Extending map matching to stationary stays](../concepts/m-map-matching-extension.md): which ideas from map matching (matching moving GPS tracks to roads) still work for a person who is standing still.
