# New GT experiment

<p class="doc-meta"><span class="chip chip-note">Note</span> <span class="chip chip-date">29-10-2026</span></p>

日本語版: [新GT実験の概要](new-gt-experiment-ja.md)

This page describes a new experiment to collect ground truth (GT) data: GPS tracks where the true place of each stop is known. It is for lab members who may take part.

## 1. Purpose

In the first experiment, participants followed a planned scenario. This time there is no scenario. Participants use their own phones and spend their time freely in a new place. This gives data closer to real life.

## 3. What you do

- Spend the days as you like. There is no fixed route and no group rule. Going alone or with others is your choice.
- Keep the app running for the whole period.
- Tap a button when a stop starts and when it ends (see 5).
- Each night, spend about 10 minutes checking the day's record (see 6).

## 4. What counts as a stop

You make a stop when you stay in one place for **5 minutes or more**. Places you only walk past are not recorded. A stop has one of three types.

| Type       | Examples                                                                                     |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Building   | Shops, restaurants, cafés, museums. Moving between floors of one building is still one stop. |
| Street     | Waiting on a sidewalk, standing at a roadside stall.                                         |
| Open space | Parks, plazas, riversides.                                                                   |

If you are not sure of the type (a covered shopping street, an underground mall, a café terrace, and so on), pick the closest type and say a few words about the place in a voice memo.

## 5. How to log

You log with [TBD: a button in the app, or a form]. Each person logs their own stops.

- Tap "start" when a stop begins and "end" when it finishes. The time is saved automatically, so you never need to check a clock and write it down.
- Choose the type (building / street / open space).
- For the place name, a voice memo of about 3 seconds is enough (optional), for example "going into Lawson". Only your own voice is recorded, for a few seconds.
- Photos keep the time they were taken, which helps when checking later (optional).
- If you forget to tap, log the stop when you notice, or add it at the evening check.

## 6. Evening check

- It takes about 10 minutes. Look at the day's GPS track on a map (how: [TBD]).
- Add any missing stops and fix any times that are off.
- Do it the same day, while you still remember.

## 7. Phone checklist

Both iPhone and Android can stop apps that run in the background. Check these before the trip.

- [ ] App installed and tested beforehand
- [ ] Location set to "always allow", precise location on
- [ ] Battery saver (battery optimisation) off for this app
- [ ] Bring a power bank
- [ ] Tell the organiser your phone model and OS version

## 8. Privacy

- You sign a consent form before taking part.
- What is collected: GPS positions and times, your taps, voice memos and photos (optional), and your phone model.
- The location of the Airbnb is hidden in the analysis.
- You can pause recording at any time. Please tell the organiser when it was paused.
