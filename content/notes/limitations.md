# Limitations

<p class="doc-meta"><span class="chip chip-note">Note</span> <span class="chip chip-date">25-07-2026</span></p>

- Controlled-protocol artificiality, especially the micro-movement stops ([Group D: movement type](experiment-group-d.md)).
- The 3 devices per stop are not independent; [Grouped cross-validation](grouped-cross-validation.md) mitigates but does not remove this.
- "Building-attached Outdoor" is merged into open space ([Self-report form](self-report-form.md)).
- The street-segmentation-driven bias means the street and open-space numbers belong to this specific layer ([Candidate density bias toward building](../analysis/candidate-density-bias.md)).
- Stays are treated independently of the participant's trip chain.
- Not yet applied to MILES, so the transfer risk in [Overfitting signature](overfitting-signature.md) is untested.
