# Motivation

<p class="doc-meta"><span class="chip chip-note">Note</span> <span class="chip chip-date">25-07-2026</span></p>

Deterministic GPS-to-entity assignment (nearest or containing building) has two structural problems:

1. It leaves out **streets and open spaces** as activity locations, although urban theory (Jacobs, Gehl, Whyte) treats them as real sites of activity ([Tension with the street-space argument](street-space-tension.md)).
2. It **ignores positional uncertainty** by forcing a single match.

Probabilistic assignment exists for moving segments (map matching), but its logic had not been extended to **stationary** stay clusters, nor combined with a multi-target building/street/open-space scope ([Extending map matching to stationary stays](map-matching-extension.md)).

Part of [Problem and positioning](../threads/problem-and-positioning.md). Leads to [Research questions](research-questions.md).
