# Study area

<p class="doc-meta"><span class="chip chip-concept">Concept</span> <span class="chip chip-project">Master</span> <span class="chip chip-date">25-07-2026</span></p>

The study takes place in **Hiroshima City**. Two practical reasons decided this: good map data exists for the city (MAPPLE streets and Zmap TOWN buildings), and the field survey was easy to organize there.

The model first learns from the [Ground truth dataset](m-ground-truth-dataset.md), the stays where the correct answer is known. After that, it is meant to be used on **MILES**, a large GPS dataset from a smartphone app. That step has not been done yet (see [Limitations](m-limitations.md)).

Part of [Ground truth data](../threads/m-ground-truth-data.md). The map layers: [Spatial entity layers](m-spatial-entity-layers.md).
